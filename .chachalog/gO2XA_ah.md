Added a second roles and permissions screen. The screen states what a role grants and where the role grants it. Each permission also carries the reason the role grants that permission. There are three reasons: the role names the permission, a parent role grants the permission, or a granted permission aggregates it.

The page of a role also states what the role is. It carries the description, the scope, the node types the role can be granted on, the privileged access and the visibility. Reading any of those needed the edit form before.

A change that reaches beyond the row is shown before it is written, and a deletion says who loses access. A role can be reset to what the installed sources declare, with the difference shown first. The current screen is unchanged, and both screens are available.
